# Route a Windows support task to its focused capability.

Select a task from its observed symptom, not a generic area label.
- Unexpected restart: [reliability/investigate-unexpected-restart](../investigate-unexpected-restart/SKILL.md).
- WSL nested virtualization: [virtualization/diagnose-wsl-kvm](../diagnose-wsl-kvm/SKILL.md).
- Disk warnings or suspected failure: [storage/assess-storage-health](../assess-windows-storage-health/SKILL.md).
- Slow login/startup load: [performance/diagnose-startup-contention](../diagnose-windows-startup-contention/SKILL.md).
- Workload contention: [performance/identify-resource-bottleneck](../identify-windows-resource-bottleneck/SKILL.md).
- Protection or policy assessment: [security/assess-security-posture](../assess-windows-security-posture/SKILL.md).
- Application/service/peripheral failure: [reliability/investigate-application-device-failure](../investigate-windows-application-device-failure/SKILL.md).
- Unclear symptom: [evidence-led-triage](../evidence-led-triage/SKILL.md).
- Uncertain prior operation: [recover-uncertain-operation](../recover-uncertain-operation/SKILL.md).
- Proposed repair: [verify-reversible-repair](../verify-reversible-repair/SKILL.md).

- Evidence for another technician: [collect-support-evidence](../collect-support-evidence/SKILL.md).

Load only the relevant owner. Area directories are organization, not executable
skills. No capability listed here grants permission to change a machine.
